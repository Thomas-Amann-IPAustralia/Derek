# Quick guides

The 6 quick guides cover common style topics. View them online or download accessible versions from each page. The accessible PDFs are designed to be printed.

- [Quick guide: common misspellings and word confusion](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-common-misspellings-and-word-confusion)
Use an Australian dictionary to find out how to spell and use words.
- [Quick guide: dates and time](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-dates-and-time)
Write dates and times consistently.
- [Quick guide: lists](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-lists)
Use lists to make it easy for users to scan and understand a series of items.
- [Quick guide: numbers](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-numbers)
Follow the rules for numbers to support accessibility and readability for users.
- [Quick guide: plain language](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-plain-language)
Write in plain language to help users understand your message.
- [Quick guide: spelling, capitals, links and dashes](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/quick-guides/quick-guide-spelling-capitals-links-and-dashes)
Read the style rules for common style topics.
